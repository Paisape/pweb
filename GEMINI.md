# Project Instructions & Style Guidelines

## Style
- Human-looking code: simple, small diffs, match the repo. No over-abstraction or AI boilerplate.
- UI: no purple gradients, Inter/Roboto, pill badges, glass cards, glow, emoji clutter. Clear hierarchy, restrained colors, natural copy.
- Hero: brand + one headline + one short line + one CTA.

## Tokens
- Be brief. Short answers. No long summaries or restating the task unless asked.
